Kasih Deepavali 2025 – Food Bag Distribution

In conjunction with the Deepavali Festival, Shechinah Association Johor Bahru and Caritas MJD Centre distributed 40 food bags with essential items and festive treats to B40 students of SMK Saint Joseph (B), Larkin Jaya.

Event Details

📅 Date: Friday, 10 October 2025
🕘 Time: 9.00 AM – 11.00 AM
📍 Venue: Dewan Jauhar, SMK Saint Joseph (B), Jalan Garuda, Larkin Jaya, Johor Bahru

Itinerary

8.00 AM
Collect goody bags from Caritas JB and proceed to SMK St. Joseph (B), Larkin
8.45 AM
Unload and arrange items at Dewan Jauhar
9.00 AM
Display items and programme setup
9.00 AM
Welcome speech by Principal En. Suhaimi & Speech by Mr. James Issachar (Chairman, Shechinah)
After speeches
Photo session
After photos
Tea break and fellowship
11.00 AM
Dismiss
Travel Note: It takes about 15 minutes to travel from Caritas JB to SMK St. Joseph, and about 20 minutes to load all the bags.
