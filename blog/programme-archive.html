2018

2018 marked the establishment of Shechinah Association Johor Bahru (Pertubuhan Kebajikan Shechinah Johor Bahru), formally incorporated on 19 April 2018.

19 April 2018
Establishment of Shechinah Association Johor Bahru
Formal incorporation as a welfare organisation serving vulnerable and underprivileged communities in Johor. Registration: PPM-006-01-19042018.
2018
Halfway House Development
Development of a Halfway House programme providing accommodation, food, counselling, prayer support, employment assistance and opportunities for vulnerable individuals to rebuild their lives.
2018
Food Assistance
Assistance with food and basic necessities for poor and vulnerable individuals and families.
2018
Prayer & Counselling
Emotional, spiritual and practical support for individuals facing difficult circumstances.
2018
Rural / Orang Asli Development
Development of outreach to rural and Orang Asli communities, particularly through education and community support.
Ongoing: Halfway House · Food Assistance · Prayer & Counselling · Rural Development

2019
Kem Remaja Orang Asli

2019
1st Annual General Meeting

2019
Orang Asli Community Outreach
Continued engagement with rural communities through education, fellowship and assistance.

2020

The COVID-19 pandemic and Movement Control Order created significant hardship among homeless people, vulnerable families and low-income communities. Shechinah responded by strengthening its food assistance and welfare outreach.

2020
Meals of Hope
Food distribution programme responding to increased needs among homeless and vulnerable people during the MCO.
2020
Homeless Feeding Programme
Food assistance provided to homeless individuals in Johor Bahru.
2020
Food Assistance for Vulnerable Families
Distribution of food and basic necessities to families affected by financial hardship.
2020
Halfway House Support
Continued accommodation, meals, counselling and practical support for residents.
2020
Community Welfare Support
Assistance to vulnerable individuals affected by the social and economic impact of COVID-19.
