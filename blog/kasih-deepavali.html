Kasih Deepavali — Sharing the Light of Hope

We were truly privileged to be invited to organize the Kasih Deepavali Food Aid Giving for the B40 community students of SMK St. Joseph, Larkin. This meaningful initiative brought together caring hearts from all walks of life to ensure that every child could experience the joy and warmth of the Deepavali season.

Our sincere appreciation goes to Teacher Kani, whose dedication and coordination made the event smooth and impactful, and to Principal En. Suhaimi, whose support opened the doors for this act of kindness.

This event was a joint collaboration between Shechinah Association Johor Bahru and Caritas MJD, with the presence of Bro. Andrew and Bro. Albert, who graced the day with their presence.

We are also deeply thankful for the generous contributions from various partners and suppliers who came forward to make this possible:

— Pemborong Sayur Jeeva
— Food Bank 448
— Pusat Jagaan Kanak-kanak Shalom Ria
— Future Foods

A special thanks to Mr. Jeeva, who not only provided goods but also took the time to inspire the students — showcasing his big bikes and sharing valuable words on motivation, hard work, and life achievements. His presence brought excitement and a sense of aspiration among the students.

Kasih Deepavali was more than just a food aid program — it was a reminder that compassion transcends backgrounds, and that the light of kindness shines brightest when shared.

As we celebrate this season of light, may every child and family touched by this effort be blessed with hope, joy, and the spirit of renewal. Together, we continue to build a Malaysia where unity and love for one another illuminate every heart.
