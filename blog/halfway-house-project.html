Halfway House Project

Today Mr. Chin was referred by Hospital Sultan Ismail (HSI) to the Halfway House.

He is 46 years old. Divorced 10 years ago. He lived in a house without electricity for 2 months.

He was admitted to HSI because of heart problems and also suffers severe depression.

We believe the Halfway House is a place of healing and renewal.

We hope that Mr. Chin will be able to work again and live a normal life in the near future.

We thank all those who have supported this project. Please feel free to contact if you are burneded to help these individuals.
