**CFLI Budget Rules to Remember:**
- No equipment over CAD $5,000 per item without justification.
- Core organisational costs (rent, utilities, admin) are generally **not** eligible unless directly tied to the project.
- All expenditures must be clearly tied to activities described in the proposal.
