## 3. Thematic Priorities (CFLI 2026 — indicative for 2027)

Based on the 2026 cycle, likely recurring themes for 2027 include:

1. **Inclusive governance, democracy, and human rights**
   - Sub-themes: civic engagement, freedom of expression, parliamentary reform, anti-corruption
2. **Peace and security**
   - Sub-themes: Women, Peace and Security (WPS); cybersecurity; countering online scam centres; maritime security; youth peacebuilding
3. **Inclusive growth and women’s economic security**
   - Sub-themes: financial inclusion, entrepreneurship, decent work, care economy
4. **Environment and climate action**
   - Sub-themes: just transition, nature-based solutions, climate adaptation, biodiversity

**Emerging / cross-cutting issues noted in 2026:**
- Artificial intelligence governance and ethics
- Online scam / trafficking prevention (relevant to Malaysia given regional scam centre crisis)
- Interfaith and multicultural cohesion

**SAJB should:** Identify which of its current or planned programmes map most credibly onto one of these four pillars. Gender equality is **mandatory** as a cross-cutting theme — every CFLI proposal must demonstrate meaningful GE integration.

## 6. Strategic Recommendations for SAJB

1. **Use the waiting period wisely.** Do not wait for the call to open. Finalise a concept note, gather Letters of Support, and prepare organisational documents now.
2. **Strengthen gender lens.** CFLI scoring heavily weights gender equality. Even if SAJB’s core work is general community aid, the proposal should articulate *how women and girls are disproportionately affected* and *how they will be centred* in project design, governance, and benefits.
3. **Consider a modest ask.** First-time CFLI recipients often secure CAD $25k–$40k. A well-scoped, achievable project is stronger than an ambitious but vague one.
4. **Engage the High Commission informally.** If SAJB can secure an introductory meeting or call before the call opens, it signals seriousness and allows SAJB to tailor the concept to officer feedback.
5. **Parallel-track corporate partnerships.** The attached "Developers with Active Foundations / CSR Divisions in Malaysia" document suggests SAJB is also exploring local corporate funding. A diversified funding pipeline strengthens SAJB’s credibility with CFLI reviewers.
