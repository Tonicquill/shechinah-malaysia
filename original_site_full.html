Introduction

Shechinah Association Johor Bahru

Shechinah Association Johor Bahru, also known as Pertutubuhan Kebajikan Shechinah Johor Bahru, was incorporated under the EROSES with a RegNo:PPM-006-01-19042018.
The association’s goals are to reach out to people in need, by providing them with a helping hand to get back up in life. No one should feel they’re hopeless, as long as there’s hope, there is an opportunity for goodness to happen. Together with the cooperation of an extraordinary team of people, we are now leading and running the Association. What started as an inner vision, has now becomea reality and a mission.

We are honoured to be a part of a great country, and a part of the wonderful state of Johor, where we are witnessing a rapid growth of a great metropolitan city.
We take pride in being part of this great state and as much as we take pride in our patriotism and devotion to our nation, we believe that we as a nation should not leave the under privileged behind. We must help them progress together with the rest, and we consider this as an ACT of PATRIOTISM.
