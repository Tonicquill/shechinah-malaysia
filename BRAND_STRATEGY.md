# Shechinah Malaysia — Brand Strategy

> Extracted from original site, Kimi prototype, and 2024-2025 news coverage
> Date: 2026-05-07

## 1. Brand Positioning

**Who we are:** Pertubuhan Kebajikan Shechinah Johor Bahru — a registered Malaysian charity (PPM-006-01-19042018) providing shelter, food, job placement, and restoration for the homeless, hungry, and forgotten across Johor Bahru since 2018.

**What we do:** Walk alongside people — former inmates, recovering addicts, Orang Asli communities, families in crisis — restoring dignity and giving hope through five core programmes.

**How we're different:** Not transactional aid. Relational restoration. James Issachar and his 50 volunteers don't just drop off supplies — they stay until people can stand on their own.

## 2. Five Brand Pillars

1. **Dignity First** — Every person deserves respect, regardless of past or circumstance
2. **Relentless Presence** — We don't stop. Started during COVID, never quit
3. **Grassroots Trust** — 10+ years in JB communities, Orang Asli villages, prison aftercare
4. **Faith in Action** — Christian charity, serves all regardless of religion
5. **Real Impact** — 6,500 shelters, 3,000 families, 4 Orang Asli villages. Numbers with names behind them

## 3. Audience Personas

**Donor (Malaysian, 30-55):** Wants to see where money goes. Responds to transparency, real stories, local impact. Prefers bank transfer or Touch 'n Go e-wallet.

**Volunteer (JB local, 20-40):** Wants to help hands-on. Needs to see programme details and contact path.

**Beneficiary / Family Member:** Needs to know services exist, location, how to reach us. No judgment, only welcome.

**Media / Partner Org:** Looking for credible story, verified stats, founder access. James's The Star coverage lends legitimacy.

## 4. Tone & Voice

| Dimension | Is | Is Not |
|-----------|-----|--------|
| Warmth | Genuine, grounded | Sentimental, saccharine |
| Authority | Lived experience, 10+ years | Institutional, bureaucratic |
| Urgency | "People need help today" | Panicked, guilt-driven |
| Faith | Implicit, through action | Preachy, exclusionary |
| Specificity | "500 meals in downtown JB" | "We help many people" |

## 5. Narrative Themes

- "Help came unexpectedly" — James's own story of being compelled to act
- "We didn't stop" — Continuity from COVID through today
- "Walk alongside" — Not saviors, companions on the journey
- "Every number has a name" — Stats are people, not metrics
- "PPM-006-01-19042018" — Legitimacy through official registration

## 6. Visual Identity Principles

- **Green = Life + Growth** — Olive green, earthy not artificial
- **Gold = Action + Hope** — Used sparingly, always points to "do something"
- **White = Fresh Start** — Clean slates, new beginnings
- **Cream = Warmth** — Only where human stories live (quotes, founder)
- **Dark = Gravity** — Final CTA, footer — the serious ask

Font: Satoshi — modern Malaysian-connected sans-serif. Clean, bold, distinctive. Not Inter/Roboto (too generic). Not serif (too traditional).

## 7. Competitive Differentiation

| | Typical NGO | Shechinah |
|---|---|---|
| Imagery | Sad children, guilt | Dignified portraits, real moments |
| Stats | Vague ("thousands helped") | Specific ("6,500 shelters since 2018") |
| Founder | Hidden / board photos | James front and center, The Star-verified |
| Ask | "Please donate" | "Be part of someone's restoration" |
| Digital | PDF reports | Living website with timeline and media |

## 8. Success Metrics

- Donation conversion from site visitors
- Volunteer inquiries
- Media/press mentions amplified through site
- Programme awareness (which ministries get most clicks)
- James Issachar personal brand elevation as credible NGO leader

## 9. Brand Governance

- Colors: CSS custom properties, single source of truth
- Font: Satoshi from CDN, fallback to system sans
- Logo: Use transparent PNG variants (original_images/)
- Photography: Real only. No stock. Gray placeholder > fake image
- Quotes: Must be attributable (name, publication, date)
